6. HYPOTHESIS TESTS  (alpha = 0.05)
========================================================================
Normality check (Shapiro-Wilk on price): p = 1.975e-11
-> Price is not normally distributed, so rank-based tests are used.

H1: price and rating are related (Spearman correlation)
    H0: no monotonic relationship
    rho = 0.029, p = 0.356 -> Fail to reject H0 (not significant)

H2: price differs across star ratings (Kruskal-Wallis)
    H0: price distributions are the same for every rating
    H = 1.564, p = 0.8152 -> Fail to reject H0 (not significant)

H3: price differs across categories with >= 10 books (Kruskal-Wallis)
    H0: price distributions are the same in every category
    H = 15.061, p = 0.9189 -> Fail to reject H0 (not significant)  (25 categories tested)

H4: description length and price are related (Spearman correlation)
    H0: no monotonic relationship
    rho = 0.061, p = 0.05366 -> Fail to reject H0 (not significant)

Note: with ~1000 rows even a tiny effect can be 'significant'. Always read rho / H together with the p-value to judge practical size.
